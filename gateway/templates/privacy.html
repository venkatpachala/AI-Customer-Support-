The account email, the policy PDF, and the case text stay on the tenant that created them. Another brand cannot read them.

Logs keep a request id, a tenant id, and a case id. They do not keep a raw card number or a full shop token. A customer reference is the id you choose, not a payment instrument.

Passwords and API keys are stored as hashes. Connection tokens are sealed and the screen shows the last four characters.
